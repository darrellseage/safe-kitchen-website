Built for chefs. Not paperwork.

Illustrative demo · not a real account · not food safety guidance
3.6°C
In the app the scale starts empty, so nobody can save a reading they didn’t take. The scale is not a safety limit, and a keypad is always available.
Your saved record appears here.
Gloves on, hands wetEvery target is at least 48px. There’s a ±0.1 button and a keypad for exact entry.
Never prefilledReadings, completed checks and actions are always entered by a person.
Who, when and how, automaticallyName, time, device and entry method are saved with each reading. No extra typing.
