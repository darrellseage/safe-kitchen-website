## 3. Per-competitor notes

### SafeTap (direct, closest competitor)
- **Verified:** £15 per site/month, cancel anytime, UK-hosted; 90-day free trial on the site (15 days on Google Play: conflicting). One-tap daily checks, fridge/freezer logs, 14-allergen matrix per dish, cleaning schedules, training records, safe methods from the SFBB pack, Bluetooth probe support, English and Welsh, offline-first, PDF export for any date range.
- **Claims to watch:** "Accepted by Environmental Health Officers." No evidence found. This is the kind of claim Safe Kitchen has chosen not to make; the site can gently make a point of that.
- **Strengths:** the same price as Safe Kitchen, already shipping, offline, allergens and probes today. **Weaknesses:** first release, no reviews found, SFBB-only scope (no rotas, training content, ordering), no visible founder/credibility story.
- **Gap Safe Kitchen can exploit (credibly):** breadth (training, rotas, clock-in, ordering in one price), the founder's kitchen experience, honesty about status, and Inspection Mode as a designed experience rather than a PDF export. **Gap SafeTap exploits against Safe Kitchen today:** it exists, works offline and has allergens; Safe Kitchen's planned-only list is long.

### Leafe
- **Verified:** Bristol, Eate Ltd. Records, HACCP, inventory, shift management with clock-in/out. Leafe Pro from £119/month with bespoke HACCP; CPD-accredited training offer for the "next 100" Pro subscribers; 7-day trial. **Reported:** £28 basic and £68 standard per venue with a 12-month contract.
- **Strengths:** closest to Safe Kitchen's all-in-one vision and already live; accredited training on the Pro tier. **Weaknesses:** contracts, tiering, Pro price is 8× Safe Kitchen; competitor claims no SFBB 4-weekly structure; "#1" claim unsupported.
- **Gap:** one price with everything, no contract, SFBB-native structure, plain honesty.

### The FSA's SFBB pack (the real incumbent)
- Free. Every council points to it. It is what most small kitchens actually use. Safe Kitchen's site currently never says "the pack is free and good; here's what paper can't do". It should.
- Any app in this space must avoid implying FSA endorsement; the FSA has publicly disclaimed SFBB+. Safe Kitchen already does this.

### SFBB+ and SFBB Pro
- SFBB+: £4.99/month per premises incl. VAT, 90-day trial, All Environmental Health Services Ltd. Pure digital pack. A price anchor at the bottom of the market: Safe Kitchen is 3× the price and must justify it with scope and experience, not with records alone.
- SFBB Pro: claims "fully verified by Government EHOs in the form of an official council partnership". Unverified; treat as a claim.

### Trail
- Verified pricing: Solo £14 (one site), Team £38, Standard £75, per site per month, unlimited users, yearly discount. Checklist-centric, strong partnerships (BII), AI assistant, integrations. Weakness for small independents: price jumps to £38 as soon as there are two sites. Safe Kitchen's flat £14.99 per site is a clear multi-site advantage.

### FoodDocs
- USD pricing from $99/site/month, HACCP builder only on $199 tier, onboarding fees. International, manufacturer-leaning. Not a realistic choice for a UK takeaway; useful as a "this is what enterprise HACCP costs" anchor.

### Checkit and Navitas Safety
- Enterprise, hardware-led (sensors, pods, probes), custom pricing, installation, multi-year contracts (reported). They set the ceiling for "automated monitoring". Safe Kitchen should not compete on sensors (vision only) and should say plainly that manual recording is the honest, inspectable baseline.

### Hubl, Lemon, Culinary Key
- Hubl: "EHO-ready from £19/month", built by consultants, AI HACCP generation and digital SFBB packs (R). Lemon: £19/location, 30-day no-card trial, unlimited team, PDF/Excel/CSV exports (R, company-written Trustpilot profile). Culinary Key: £40 per user. These confirm the £15–£20 per site price band is where UK independents are being sold to, and that **"unlimited staff" is table stakes, not a differentiator** (Trail, Lemon and FoodDocs all say it).

## 4. What this means for Safe Kitchen

1. **£14.99 is at the bottom of the realistic band (£14–£20), not uniquely cheap.** SafeTap £15, Trail Solo £14, Hubl £19, Lemon £19. SFBB+ at £4.99 is cheaper. The price is credible for a single site; it is a **strong** advantage against Trail/Leafe for 2+ sites and against everyone on included training, if training ships.
2. **"Unlimited staff" is standard.** Say it, but don't lead with it.
3. **Everyone claims EHO readiness; two claim EHO acceptance or verification without evidence.** Safe Kitchen's honesty is a real, defensible difference, and the pilot with four restaurants is the credible path to proof.
4. **Offline and allergens are expected by the cheapest direct rival.** Both are planned for Safe Kitchen. The site must not pretend otherwise, and the roadmap should prioritise them.
5. **Design:** none of the small UK competitors has a distinctive brand. The field is utility apps and bright consumer templates. A confident, kitchen-native identity is open ground.
6. **The incumbent is paper.** The site should respect the free FSA pack and sell the difference: finding, proving and doing it in one place.
