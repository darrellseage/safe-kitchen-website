## 2. Principles, applied

- **Clear value proposition in the first screen:** category + outcome + price. "Digital SFBB records and kitchen management, built by a head chef. From £14.99 + VAT per site, unlimited staff."
- **Specificity:** real times, real temperatures (sample), real prices with VAT, the founder's real roles. No "save hours every week" without the calculator's own caveat.
- **Demonstration over assertion:** keep the temperature demo and Inspection Mode demo near the top; move planned-feature demos lower and label them.
- **Risk reduction:** free early access, no card, no commitment; a pilot with four kitchens before launch; honest status; export promised as a requirement; no contract.
- **Credible authority:** the founder's experience, stated factually; the SFBB basis stated without implying endorsement; a line on why "EHO approved" is not claimed.
- **Transparent pricing:** both prices, both with VAT, per site, unlimited staff, no set-up fee, what's never charged. One place, plus the hero line.
- **Objection handling:** a short "Straight answers" block near pricing, not a long FAQ.
- **Social proof:** none exists; say so and make the pilot the story ("Four kitchens are testing it before anyone pays. Be one of the first to see it.").
- **Simple calls to action:** one primary ("Join early access", free) and one secondary ("Book a call"). Four placements.
- **Reduced cognitive load:** 8–9 sections, under 1,800 words above the FAQ, one idea per section.

**Never:** fake scarcity, invented testimonials, invented customer counts, countdowns, "trusted by 500 kitchens", "EHO approved", "guarantees a 5".

## 5. Revised funnel

1. **Land** (search, word of mouth, trade group, delivery-app forums) → hero passes the five-second test.
2. **Recognise** (section 5) and **believe** (sections 3–4 demos).
3. **Price check** (section 7) with no surprises.
4. **Trust** (section 7 straight answers, section 8 founder).
5. **Act**: Step 1 of the form (email + choice) saved immediately. Step 2 optional for early access.
6. **Nurture** (outside the site): a personal email from Darrell within a day; a call for multi-site; pilot invitations to the first suitable kitchens.
7. **Measure** (Stage 5): hero CTA clicks, demo interactions, pricing views, form step 1 and step 2 completions, call bookings. No analytics until the consent banner exists.

## 6. Pricing positioning (Phase 7 of the brief)

- **Where Safe Kitchen is stronger:** against Trail (£38/site from the second site), Leafe (£68 standard with contract; £119 Pro), FoodDocs ($99+), and every per-user product (Culinary Key £40/user, Checkit ~£20/user). Included training (when shipped) beats Leafe's promotional offer and everyone else's silence. No contract beats Leafe's 12 months.
- **Where competitors are stronger:** SFBB+ at £4.99 for a pure digital pack; SafeTap at £15 already shipping with offline, allergens and probes; Lemon's 30-day no-card trial today. Enterprise tools have sensors and installation.
- **Does £14.99 create a quality concern?** At the bottom of the £14–£20 band, slightly. Answer it in one sentence: one founder, no sales team, per site not per head, and the price locks for founding customers (when announced).
- **Is annual attractive enough?** £29.89 (16.6%) is standard. It's enough; don't discount further. Say "about two months free".
- **Unlimited staff:** state it, don't lead with it (every rival has it).
- **Included training:** lead with it once training is real; until then it's "planned" and should not be the headline.
- **Comparison table:** yes, but only of *billing models and scope*, with no competitor prices on the page (they change and invite disputes). "Per site, not per person. No contract. Training included." against "per user / contract / training extra" as generic columns.
- **Savings calculator:** keep one, behind a link, with editable assumptions and the existing caveats. Not a section, and never a headline number.

## 7. Recommended copy (ready to use)

- Hero: see #1. Price line: "From £14.99 a month per site + VAT (£17.99 incl. VAT). Unlimited staff. No set-up fee." Status line: "In development. Four kitchens are testing it before anyone pays."
- Inspection: "No warning. Lunch prep is under way. The manager opens Inspection Mode and the last 28 days are on screen, with anything missing shown plainly. Organised records won't do the inspection for you. They mean you're not hunting for them."
- Why no "EHO approved": "Some apps say they're accepted or approved by Environmental Health Officers. There's no such scheme. Your records are judged on whether the checks were done. Safe Kitchen is built so they can be shown in seconds and never faked."
- Paper: "The FSA's SFBB pack is free and good. What paper can't do is tell you at 10pm that an afternoon reading is missing, or show an inspector last month in four seconds."
- Head chef: "It checks the paperwork, not your team."
- Pilot: "Four kitchens, before launch. If you'd like yours to be one of them, say so on the form."
