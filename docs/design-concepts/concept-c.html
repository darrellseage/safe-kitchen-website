The three things that keep owners up

Inspection day. Five systems. Hours you don't have.

Takeaway & restaurant owners

The knock at the back door.

Your hygiene rating is on the door and online. Safe Kitchen is being built so your records are always ready to show, with anything missing shown honestly. It can't guarantee a rating. It can make sure you're never searching for last month.

Head chefs

Paper in a wet kitchen.

Big buttons that work with gloves on. Readings are never filled in for you, and a bad reading is as quick to record as a good one. The AI Checker, when it arrives, looks at records, never at people.

Managers

Chasing, then chasing again.

Rota from a photo, clock-in on the phone, orders checked for duplicates before they go, and one daily summary of what needs attention.

Multi-site operators

Different standards at every site.

One view across your sites. Priced per site, never per person, so adding a kitchen is one line, not a renegotiation.

Built around service, not around a desk.

Every chef-facing check in Safe Kitchen is designed to take five to ten seconds. Management tools stay out of the kitchen's way. Missing records are never hidden, and the original entry is never overwritten.
