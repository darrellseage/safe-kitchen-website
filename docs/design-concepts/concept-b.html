Inspections

The visit nobody books.

An inspection isn't a test of your cooking. It's a test of whether you can show, on the spot, that the checks happened. Safe Kitchen is designed so that showing takes seconds: open Inspection Mode and the last 28 days are on screen, with anything missing shown plainly. It can't guarantee a rating, and it won't do the inspection for you. It means you're not hunting through folders while the food is going cold.

"The EHO wants last month's records. The manager is in the office, searching through folders for the right week."The moment we're designing against · not a testimonial
